Executive summary

A platform for focused transformation

The results point to meaningful strengths in learner experience, research and partnership capacity — alongside a clear need to build the leadership systems, resources and infrastructure that make experimentation possible. The assessment is best read as a portfolio: it shows where NDSU can scale existing momentum, where it should prototype new approaches and where sequencing matters.

Methodology

How to read this assessment

The dashboard brings three aggregate measures into one decision frame. It preserves the original six-imperative structure and four strategic quadrants while protecting the assessment's respondent-level data.

01

Design Imperatives

Statement means use the assessment's five-point scale. Each imperative score is the average of its mapped statement means, calculated from the six original Design Imperative sections.

02

Strategic priorities

The 16 priorities use the aggregate Borda-style adjusted score selected for this project. Higher scores indicate greater collective priority. The matrix threshold is 8.5.

03

Capacity dimensions

Capacity is the aggregate average across Funding, Talent, Systems & Infrastructure, Experience and Partnerships. Higher scores indicate greater current capacity. The matrix threshold is 3.0.

04

Interpretation

Results are directional and designed to support institutional dialogue, sequencing and portfolio decisions. They should be considered with qualitative evidence, feasibility and strategic judgment.

Prototype

High priority, lower current capacity. Test, learn and build what is missing.

Scale

High priority, higher current capacity. Accelerate and extend existing momentum.

Leverage

Lower priority, higher current capacity. Use selectively or as an enabler.

Defer

Lower priority, lower current capacity. Sequence later or revisit as conditions change.
